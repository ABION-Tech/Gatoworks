Questions, answered

Cannot find yours? Message us and we will reply.

What does Gatoworks do?

We are a creative house covering art consulting, African textile and fashion design, home décor and printing.

Where are you located?

We have two spaces in Abuja: Suite 320, Turai Yar'Adua Block, Wuye Ultra Modern Market, Wuye; and Suite 75A, Arts & Craft Village, opposite Abuja Intercontinental Hotel, CBD.

How do I place an order?

Pick a piece in the Shop and tap Ask on WhatsApp. We confirm details, price and delivery before you pay.

Can you make something custom?

Yes. Custom clothing, art, décor and prints are all available. Send your idea, size or reference photos and we will quote you.

How long does a custom order take?

It depends on the item and quantity. We give you a timeline when we confirm your order.

Do you deliver outside Abuja?

Ask us when you order. We confirm delivery options and cost for your location before you pay.
