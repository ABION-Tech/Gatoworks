Rooms with a point of view

A good room feels calm, personal and easy to live in. We pick art, textiles and furnishings that work together, and for bigger statements we design and build details such as ceiling panels, doorways and decorative walls.

We work with homeowners, offices and venues, from a single feature wall to a complete interior.

Look up. The ceiling is a canvas too.

We design and install geometric ceilings, calligraphy panels and decorative doorways.

Living spaces

Welcoming rooms with art, texture and good lighting at the centre. We balance colour, furniture and wall pieces so the room feels collected, not crowded.

  • Art and gallery walls
  • Shelving, lighting and accessories
  • Colour and texture planning

Bedrooms

Soft, layered and restful, with details that feel like you. We choose wall art, textiles and lighting to make the room somewhere you want to end the day.

  • Wall art above the bed
  • Cushions, throws and textiles
  • Warm, low lighting

Statement walls, ceilings and doors

Geometric panels and gold calligraphy built into the architecture, designed and installed by our team. These details turn an ordinary entrance into a memorable one.

  • Calligraphy and lattice panels
  • Domed and geometric ceilings
  • Arched doorways and facades

Arches and entrances

Black panels, gold lettering and a carved arch turn a doorway into the first thing people remember. We plan the lettering, pattern and finish to suit the architecture.

  • Arabic calligraphy panels
  • Gold and black detailing
  • Marble and plaster walls

Geometric wall murals

Blue, gold and white patterns built from stars and tiles, painted by hand and scaled to the wall. Ideal for prayer rooms, reception areas and living spaces.

  • Islamic geometric patterns
  • Custom colour palettes
  • Clean, sharp edges

Art along the way

Hallways and stairs are where art works hardest. One large portrait or a small cluster can lift a plain corridor.

  • Placement and spacing advice
  • Lighting suggestions
  • Framing and hanging
