Art consult

Expert guidance on choosing, commissioning and hanging art for your space.

Start on WhatsApp How it works

Art that belongs in your room

Buying art can feel like guesswork. We take the guesswork out. Tell us about the room, the light, the mood you want and the budget, and we help you find pieces that feel like they were always meant to be there.

You can browse original paintings in our Abuja showrooms, see work in progress, or start on WhatsApp and we will send options. We also help offices and venues build a collection over time.

One good piece changes a whole room.

Colour, scale and subject set the mood before anyone sits down.

Selection

We shortlist pieces that suit your walls, colours and taste, so you choose from a few right options instead of hundreds. We explain the artist, the medium and why each piece works.

  • Paintings in oil, acrylic and mixed media
  • Portraits, wildlife, street life and abstracts
  • Advice on scale and placement

Commissions

Have something made for you. We connect you with artists and agree size, subject and style before work starts, so the final piece is what you imagined.

  • Portraits of people and families
  • Pieces for homes, offices and venues
  • Progress photos along the way

Framing and hanging

Proper framing and careful installation make a painting look finished and keep it safe. We advise on frames, spacing and lighting, and help you hang it.

  • Framing to suit the piece and the room
  • Gallery-style groupings
  • Delivery and installation

  • Two Abuja showrooms: Wuye and CBD

How to choose a piece

Start with the wall

Measure the space. A large wall can take one bold canvas; a small one suits a pair.

Read the light

Bright rooms handle dark, heavy colour. Dim rooms come alive with warm, light work.

Trust the pull

Choose what you keep looking at. That is the piece you will still love in ten years.

Good to know

Do I need to know about art?

No. Tell us what you like and what the room needs. We do the rest and explain our choices.

Can I see pieces in person?

Yes. Visit either Abuja showroom, or ask us to send photos and videos first.

Can you work to a budget?

Yes. Tell us your budget at the start and we will shortlist within it.
