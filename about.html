About Gatoworks

At the intersection of creativity and excellence, we bring ideas to life.

Led by Abdulakeem Ajani

Gatoworks Horizon Ltd is a creative house fusing art, interiors, fashion and print. Our founder and CEO, Abdulakeem Ajani, leads a studio and two spaces in Abuja.

Mission. To inspire, create and innovate solutions that help businesses and individuals reach their potential.

Vision. To be a global leader in creative solutions, delivering value to clients and communities.

We keep two spaces in Abuja where you can see work in person, talk through a project and collect your orders. Come by, or message us first so we can have pieces ready for you.
